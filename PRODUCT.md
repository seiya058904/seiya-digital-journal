# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors exploring a personal digital journal and its visual archive. They browse published work, leave a short thought through the existing journal stepper, and can add to the archive's public like count.

## Product Purpose

Seiya is a dark, editorial personal digital journal that turns creative work and personal growth into an explorable public archive.

## Brand Personality

Atmospheric, tactile, and introspective. The interface should feel crafted and quietly expressive rather than like a generic dashboard or social network.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS layouts, fabricated testimonials, user-profile-like like systems, and decorative UI that changes the meaning of the journal. Comments remain real visitor notes, and likes remain a simple public counter.

## Design Principles

- Let the archive content remain the focus.
- Use motion and texture to create atmosphere, not friction.
- Keep public reading and private comment writing clearly separated.
- Prefer small, understandable interactions over identity-heavy systems.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep keyboard focus visible, preserve semantic headings and live feedback for asynchronous actions, maintain readable contrast, and provide reduced-motion behavior for every animation.
